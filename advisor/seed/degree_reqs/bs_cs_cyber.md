# BS in Computer Science — Cybersecurity certificate

**Program ID:** `bs_cs_cyber` | **Total degree credits:** 123 | **Catalog year:** 2026

## Overview

UTA's BS in Computer Science (ABET-accredited) combined with the undergraduate Certificate in Cybersecurity, which UTA awards concurrently with the degree. UTA does not offer a formal 'concentration'; the certificate is the cybersecurity track. The certificate needs CSE 4344 (or 4352), 4380, 4381 and 4382 (12 hrs, 3.0 GPA on those courses). In the BS CS, CSE 4344 and one security course are required and there are six technical electives, so the certificate fits inside the degree with no extra hours when certificate courses may double-count (unconfirmed).

- CSE 4344: CSE 3320 — minimum grade C or better

- CSE 4382: CSE 3320 — minimum grade C or better

## Advising notes

- Admission to the Engineering Professional Program is required before taking most 3000/4000-level CSE courses (see the `professional_courses` table). Requires a 2.5 GPA overall, in required science/math/engineering courses, and in required CSE courses, with no more than four unsuccessful engineering attempts.
- All prerequisites for professional-program courses must be completed with a C or better.
- Certificate courses need a combined 3.0 GPA. Certificate course substitutions need advance approval from the certificate coordinator.
- Course offerings: 1000/2000-level CSE courses are offered every term including summer; 3000-level and required 4000-level courses at least twice a year; other 4000-level electives about once a year.
- Sample courses shown for general-core buckets come from UTA's 2025-26 general core list; any course on the current approved list counts.
- UNCONFIRMED: this plan assumes certificate courses also count toward degree requirements. Confirm with the CSE undergraduate advisors (cseugadvising@uta.edu).
