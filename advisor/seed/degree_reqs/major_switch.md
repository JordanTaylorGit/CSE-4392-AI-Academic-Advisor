# Switching between BS Software Engineering (AI certificate) and BS Computer Science (Cybersecurity certificate)

**Program IDs:** `bs_se_ai`, `bs_cs_cyber` | **Catalog year:** 2026

The two degrees share all general-core, math/science and CSE foundation courses, and the catalog's recommended four-year plans are identical for the first four semesters (first two years), so a switch after sophomore year loses no first- or second-year credit. Totals: BS SE + AI certificate = 127 hrs; BS CS + Cybersecurity certificate = 123 hrs.

## From BS SE + AI certificate to BS CS + Cybersecurity certificate

- Completed courses that stop being required but can count as technical electives: CSE 3311, CSE 4308, CSE 4309, CSE 4310, CSE 4311, CSE 4321, CSE 4322, CSE 4361 (only 4 technical-elective slots plus 1 open elective are available, so not all of them can be used).
- Newly required courses: CSE 4344, CSE 4381, CSE 4382.
- Gains 4 free technical-elective slots and 1 open elective; loses the 4-hr science elective (how a completed science elective is credited should be confirmed with an advisor).

## From BS CS + Cybersecurity certificate to BS SE + AI certificate

- Completed courses with no matching slot in the new program: CSE 4344, CSE 4381, CSE 4382 (the new program has no free technical-elective slots because its certificate uses them; an advisor may still allow them as open electives).
- Newly required courses: CSE 3311, CSE 4308, CSE 4309, CSE 4310, CSE 4311, CSE 4321, CSE 4322, CSE 4361.
- Gains the 4-hr science elective; the four technical electives and the open elective are replaced by the AI certificate courses, and one AI course is an extra 3 hrs.

UNCONFIRMED: these comparisons assume certificate courses may double-count toward degree requirements; confirm with cseugadvising@uta.edu.
