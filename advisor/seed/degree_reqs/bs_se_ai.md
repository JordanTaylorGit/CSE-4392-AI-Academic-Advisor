# BS in Software Engineering — Concentration in Artificial Intelligence

**Program ID:** `bs_se_ai` | **Total degree credits:** 120 | **Catalog year:** 2026

## Overview

The Bachelor of Science in Software Engineering with a concentration in
Artificial Intelligence combines rigorous software-engineering practice —
requirements, architecture, testing, and project management — with modern AI
coursework in machine learning, intelligent systems, and data-driven
applications.

## University Core Curriculum (42 hours)

English composition (6 hrs), US history (6 hrs), government/political science
(6 hrs), social & behavioral science (3 hrs), creative arts (3 hrs), language
philosophy & culture (3 hrs), component area option (3 hrs), and additional
mathematics/science hours satisfied by the math & science requirements below.

## Mathematics & Science (18 hours)

- MATH 1426 — Calculus I (4)
- MATH 2425 — Calculus II (4)
- MATH 2326 — Calculus III (4)
- MATH 3330 — Matrix Methods / Linear Algebra (3)
- Natural science electives with labs (e.g. PHYS 1443, PHYS 1444) (3+)

## CSE / SE Core (30 hours)

- CSE 1105 — Introduction to Computer Science and Engineering
- CSE 1310 — Introduction to Computers and Programming
- CSE 1320 — Intermediate Programming
- CSE 1325 — Object-Oriented Programming
- CSE 2312 — Computer Organization and Assembly Language Programming
- CSE 2315 — Discrete Structures
- CSE 3310 — Fundamentals of Software Engineering
- CSE 3315 — Theoretical Concepts in Computer Science
- CSE 3318 — Algorithms and Data Structures
- CSE 3354 — Software Requirements and Design
- CSE 4351 / CSE 4352 — Software Engineering Project I & II (senior capstone)

## Artificial Intelligence Concentration (15 hours)

- CSE 4308 — Artificial Intelligence (required)
- CSE 4309 — Fundamentals of Machine Learning (required)
- CSE 4310 — Neural Networks and Deep Learning (required)
- Two approved AI electives, e.g. CSE 4303 (data structures applications /
  NLP elective), CSE 4342 (embedded intelligent systems), or an approved
  CSE 4000-level elective.

## Technical Electives & Free Electives (15 hours)

Approved CSE 4000-level electives and free electives to reach 120 total hours.

## Advising notes

- AI electives often require MATH 3330 (linear algebra); students should
  complete it before CSE 4309.
- The capstone sequence (4351 -> 4352) must be taken in consecutive semesters.
- A minimum grade of C is required in all CSE courses used toward the degree.
- Total recommended credit hours may not exceed the student's per-semester
  maximum and may not exceed 120 total degree hours.
